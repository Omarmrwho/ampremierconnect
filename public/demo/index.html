The Studio

Not just decor. A complete event environment.

Divine Designs & Events creates distinctive environments for companies, venues, hospitality groups, fashion and beauty brands, luxury hosts, and milestone celebrations. The work blends floral design, ambiance, interior styling, lighting, draping, and production details into a complete guest experience.

Led by Master Floral Designer Maha Abuagel, the studio brings international design influence and high-touch client service to events that need more than decoration. The room should carry the mood, the story, and the standard of the occasion before the first guest says a word.
